# Thesio
A local-first workspace for academic research and writing, bringing notes, references, citations, and professional typesetting together in one seamless experience.

## Problem Space
Writing scientific papers has always been a hassle, the situation alone is already stressful enough and the toolchain works against it. You tend to have Obsidian (or Notion), Zotero and an app for writing the actual paper (Overleaf or others).
There is no good tool which combines everything in one.

## The Solution
Thesio combines all three aspects into one
- citation management
- notetaking
- writing the paper

### Technicalities
- For citation, we are going to use the bibtex format as export.
- For notetaking & writing, we use the typst document format, https://typst.app/
